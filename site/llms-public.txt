# superbot

> superbot is an agent manager app.
> It manages the AI apps you already pay for, like Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, ChatGPT and Gemini.
> It never wraps or replaces them.

superbot is in private beta.
Access is by invite.

## What it is

- An agent manager, the way 1Password is a password manager.
- A desktop app laid out like Discord, with one icon per connected AI app.
- Chats from every provider sync to every device you link.
- The Helper is a small relay on your computer that holds your AI app credentials.
- `superbot` is the Helper's command line tool.
- Superbot Fast, the house model, is free during the beta.

## Platforms

- macOS: the only downloadable build today.
- iOS: TestFlight, for beta testers.
- Android: Google Play internal testing, for beta testers.
- Windows and Linux: no build yet.

## Getting access

- [Private beta](https://superbot.gg/invite): sign in with an invite code.
- [Waitlist](https://superbot.gg/): the "get an invite" form on the home page.
- [@superbot_gg on X](https://x.com/superbot_gg): invite codes drop there first.
- [Download](https://beta.superbot.gg/download): opens once you are in the beta.
