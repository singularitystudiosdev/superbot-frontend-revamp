What you'll get

Once you text in, Superbot replies to what you send: answers to the questions you ask and updates on the tasks you asked it to run, plus a one-time confirmation text when you link a Superbot account by SMS.

These are not marketing or promotional messages. Every reply is a direct response to something you asked, or a service message tied to your own request.

Replies are generated by an AI and can make mistakes, so check anything important before you act on it.
