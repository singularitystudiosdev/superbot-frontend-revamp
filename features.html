super mode

one ask gets a team of agents.
they run on your subscriptions.
a manager splits the work.
each worker reports its cost.

daemon mode

it keeps watching and never quits.
it checks back on a slowing clock.
it parks on a time, file or command.
it picks back up when that is true.
resume it now, or cancel the wait.

what we can’t see

logins are sealed locally.
only your devices hold the key.
we can read a session, not a login.
we never train a model on your chats.
turn off any import, or pause them all.

synced chats are encrypted at rest.
unlike sign-ins, we hold that key.
