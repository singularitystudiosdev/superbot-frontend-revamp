superbot.gg privacy notice

Version 2026-10-07. What superbot collects, where it goes and how long we keep it. For superbot, this notice replaces the general EZO Labs Inc privacy policy at ezolabs.io.

The short version

  • Who we are. EZO Labs Inc is the controller.
  • Why. To sign you in, sync, run requests, bill you and stay secure.
  • What we keep. Your account, payments, uploads, logs, synced and imported chats, which we can read.
  • Sealed on your device. Passwords, sessions and SSH keys, read by default. We cannot read them. Turn any off in Settings.
  • Chat text. Always kept 180 days to fix bugs, including model calls the Helper relays. Gateway prompts about 15 days.
  • No training. Superbot never trains a model on your chats (details).
  • Not sold. We do not sell your personal data (details).
  • Your rights. Get a copy, or have us correct, delete or move your data, at /account or privacy@superbot.gg.

In detail: account and billing, device reading, connections, chats and files, calls and teams, logs, who processes it and your rights.

3. What the Helper reads

In new installs, the Helper reads from your browsers, password managers, ~/.ssh folder and other AI apps by default. Each is a separate setting, and you can turn any of them off in Settings > Privacy:

  • Session import: browser cookies and sessions, and whether you are signed in to a short list of sites.
  • Password scan: saved passwords and SSH private keys.
  • Chat import: your chat histories from other AI apps.
  • Config import: the MCP servers set up in your other apps.

Cookies, sessions, passwords and SSH keys are sealed on your device with a key only your devices hold before they sync. Our servers store the sealed copy and cannot open it. Chat histories and the list of signed-in sites are not sealed, and we can read them.

How often each runs, where it goes and how long we keep it is under what the Helper reads from your device. Turning a setting off stops future reads. Delete what was already synced in your vault. An existing install keeps any answer you already gave.

4. Cookies

superbot sets only these first-party cookies: sbk, sbfl, sblk, sbnh, sbinv, sbp, sbsi, sbfn, sb_ghlink_csrf, sbev, sbu, _ga. Strictly necessary: sbk holds your sign-in session for up to 90 days; sbfl, sblk, sbnh, sbinv, sbp, sbsi, sbfn, sb_ghlink_csrf only carry the state of a sign-in, invitation or account-linking step and expire within 1 day. Analytics: sbev, sbu, _ga, which last 2 minutes and 180 days and 13 months. There are no advertising cookies. We also keep first-party values in your browser's local and session storage for preferences, page state and our own landing-page experiment. None is shared with advertisers. The only ones that leave your browser are sb-attr, sb-vid, sb-sid, sb-internal, sb-ga-force: our own pages read sb-attr, sb-vid, sb-sid, and Google Analytics reads sb-attr, sb-internal, sb-ga-force. Clearing cookies signs you out.

Google Analytics

Our web pages (superbot.gg, beta.superbot.gg and this site) may use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and sign-up steps: the pages you view, with invite codes, one-time codes, tokens and email addresses removed from every address before it is sent, the links and buttons you click, your approximate location and your browser and device type. Email addresses and codes are never sent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland analytics storage is off by default, so Google Analytics sets no _ga cookie there. All of Google's advertising features are off: no Google signals, no ad personalization, no ad storage. The apps do not use Google Analytics. To opt out on any site, install Google's opt-out browser add-on.

5. Who else processes your data

Each processor receives only what its purpose needs:

  • Stripe: payments, subscriptions and auto-recharge.
  • Resend: sign-in codes, invitations and account email.
  • Sentry: app crash and error reports, without your account or device identity.
  • Apple: push notifications, and TestFlight invitations, which receive your Apple ID email.
  • Expo: mobile push notifications.
  • Cloudflare: the network in front of our service, and the router and analytics behind superbot.sh addresses.
  • Our hosting providers: the servers that run our service and our house model.
  • Telnyx: calls, SMS and fax. Twilio: text messages in shared rooms.
  • Deepgram: speech-to-text for voice sessions and cloud dictation, which receives your live microphone audio.
  • ElevenLabs: voices for voice sessions and calls, voice cloning, and generated sound and music.
  • Model vendors: Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Google, Meta, DeepSeek and others receive the prompt of a turn we route to them. Some connections use a shared client identity that is not registered to superbot. Our house model is self-hosted and falls back to Anthropic.
  • Media vendors: OpenRouter, fal, OpenAI, Google, xAI, Meshy, Tripo and ElevenLabs receive the prompt for an image, video, audio clip or 3D model, under our own accounts with them.
  • Search engines: the web-search tool sends a query's search terms to Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave and Google News. Your account details are not sent.

7. Your rights, and how to make a request

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct, delete, restrict or move it, or object to a use of it. Use the export and delete controls at /account, or email privacy@superbot.gg (also if you cannot sign in).

Export your data at /account gives you a copy of your account and sign-in data, your synced data (chats with the full text of every message, projects, rules, skills, context documents, MCP servers, settings, spaces, teams, shares and access grants), your phone and text records, your calls, your billing account (balance, plan and credit buckets), your usage records, payment events and deletion requests, your purchase contracts with the waiver you gave for each (what you bought, what you paid, its payment reference, your answer to the 14-day waiver and any refund), and the records that survive deletion (call and text consent, STOP opt-outs and charge records). Your conversation turns, relay messages, device events and bug reports are capped at 1,000 of the newest rows of each. Your phone calls, phone messages, text log, usage records and purchase contracts are capped at 5,000 of the newest rows of each. A field your device stored under a name that marks it as a secret, such as a token, hash or password, is left out of the copy. Rows stored without your account are not in it.

The export includes sign-in data.

Requests are free, and we may confirm your email first. We answer within 30 days in the EEA and the UK (GDPR Art. 12(3)) and within 45 days everywhere else (CCPA §1798.130). We extend only by telling you before the deadline. You can also complain to your data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EEA, your national supervisory authority (GDPR Art. 77, DPA 2018 s165).

We do not sell your personal data, and the only advertising report we make is the signup an ad brought us: the ad platform named in section 5 receives a hashed signup email, the ad click id and, for Reddit, the browser's user agent. Nobody else receives your data for advertising. We may make de-identified or aggregated data from how superbot is used, data that cannot reasonably be linked to you or your household, and use, share or sell it. We keep that data de-identified, do not try to re-identify it, and require anyone who receives it to promise the same in writing (CCPA §1798.140(m)). Before we first sell any, we email you as section 11 describes.

If you are not a superbot user. Data about you can reach us, for example as the author of a post that built an app, or in a call or message someone sent you. You can ask us for a copy of it, or to correct or delete it, at privacy@superbot.gg (GDPR Art. 14).

8. Automated decisions

No decision about you, such as your billing, your account standing or deleting your data, is made solely by automated processing with a legal or similarly significant effect (GDPR Art. 22). If an automated step gets something wrong, ask us through section 7 and a person will review it.

9. Children

superbot is for people aged 13 or older, and you must be 18 or older to buy. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, write to privacy@superbot.gg and we will delete it.

10. Security

Only your devices can read

Sealed on your device under a key only your devices hold, so we cannot read them:

  • browser sessions
  • saved passwords
  • SSH keys
  • imported MCP headers
  • publishing grants
  • team vault items
  • model sign-ins sealed on an enrolled device

We can read

Encrypted at rest under keys our servers hold:

  • synced chats and settings
  • pasted API and endpoint keys
  • a copy of each superbot API key you create
  • Git tokens (sent to your account so your phone can list your repositories)
  • MCP headers you add by hand
  • connected-account tokens
  • project mirrors

Stored as readable data:

  • imported chat histories
  • logs and telemetry

If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we tell the supervisory authority within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay (GDPR Art. 33 and 34). Report a security issue to security@superbot.gg.

11. Changes

We may change this notice at any time, and publish each change here with a new version date. A minor change, such as a correction or a clarification, takes effect when we publish it.

A material change is emailed to you before it takes effect: at least 30 days before if you live in the EEA or the UK, and at least 7 days before everywhere else. Material changes include any new recipient or processor of your data, any new transfer of it out of the EEA or the UK, any new purpose, any new class of data and any longer retention. The email links to the new version, says what changes and the date it takes effect. Where the law needs your consent, we ask for it.

If you do not accept a material change, you can delete your data or account before it takes effect, and a consumer can end a paid plan free of charge with the refund in section 12 of the terms.
